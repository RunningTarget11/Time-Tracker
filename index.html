Install on your phone

Android (Chrome): tap the ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen). It then opens full-screen like a regular app and works offline.

How time is counted

  • Everything is in 6-minute increments (0.1 hour).
  • The app reads today's date from your phone and opens on that day.
  • Time beyond your shift length (set in Profile) goes to Extra hours.
  • Your data stays on this phone. Use Backup to save a copy.
